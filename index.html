About Keegan

Keegan is a trombonist studying music at Butler University. He loves the full range of what the instrument can do, from a big orchestral chorale to a lyrical solo line to the back row of a swinging big band.

He started playing in middle school band and hasn't put the horn down since. At Butler he performs with the school's ensembles, plays in chamber groups, and is available for gigs around Indianapolis: weddings, church services, pit orchestras and brass quintet work.
